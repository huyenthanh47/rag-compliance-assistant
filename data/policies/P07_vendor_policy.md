# P07: Vendor and Third-Party Risk Policy
Northside Family Clinic (fictional organization; synthetic policy written for a course project, not legal advice)

## P07-7.1 Business Associate Agreement
No vendor may access, store or process patient information until the vendor has signed a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with the clinic.

## P07-7.2 Vendor security review
IT Security reviews each vendor before a contract is signed and again every year. A vendor that handles patient information must provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or an equivalent independent assessment.

## P07-7.3 Vendor access
Vendors receive only the minimum access they need. Vendor accounts expire after 90 days unless the business owner renews them.

## P07-7.4 Vendor incident reporting
A vendor must notify the clinic within 48 hours of discovering a breach or security incident that involves clinic patient information.

## P07-7.5 End of contract
When a contract ends, the vendor must return all patient information or certify in writing that it has been destroyed, within 30 days of the contract end date.

## P07-7.6 Artificial intelligence tools
Staff must not enter patient information into public AI chatbots or any other AI tool unless the tool is covered by a signed BAA and has been approved in writing by the Security Officer.
