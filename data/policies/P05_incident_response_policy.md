# P05: Incident Response and Breach Notification Policy
Northside Family Clinic (fictional organization; synthetic policy written for a course project, not legal advice)

## P05-5.1 Definition of a security incident
A security incident is any suspected or confirmed unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of clinic information or systems, including lost devices and phishing emails that were clicked.

## P05-5.2 Reporting an incident
Employees must report a suspected incident immediately and no later than 1 hour after noticing it. Reports go to the Security Officer at extension 5000 or at security@northsidefamilyclinic.example.

## P05-5.3 Risk assessment
The Privacy Officer completes a four-factor risk assessment within 5 business days of a report to decide whether the incident is a reportable breach of unsecured patient information.

## P05-5.4 Notifying patients
If a breach is confirmed, each affected patient must be notified without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after the breach was discovered.

## P05-5.5 Notifying regulators and media
A breach affecting 500 or more individuals must be reported to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and to the media at the same time as the individual notices, within 60 days of discovery. Breaches affecting fewer than 500 individuals are logged and reported to HHS within 60 days after the end of the calendar year.

## P05-5.6 No retaliation
Employees who report a suspected incident in good faith will not be punished or retaliated against.
