# P01: Email and Electronic Communication Policy
Northside Family Clinic (fictional organization; synthetic policy written for a course project, not legal advice)

## P01-1.1 Purpose and scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and volunteers of Northside Family Clinic. It covers email, text messaging, fax and file-sharing services used to handle clinic information.

## P01-1.2 Emailing patient information
Patient records and any information that identifies a patient (electronic protected health information, ePHI) must never be sent to personal email accounts such as Gmail, Yahoo or Outlook.com. ePHI may be emailed only between clinic addresses ending in @northsidefamilyclinic.example. To send ePHI to an outside recipient, such as a specialist, staff must use the clinic's encrypted email system by typing [SECURE] at the start of the subject line.

## P01-1.3 Minimum necessary
Staff must include only the minimum necessary information in any message. A patient's name, date of birth or diagnosis must never appear in an email subject line.

## P01-1.4 Misdirected email
If a message containing ePHI is sent to the wrong recipient, the sender must report it to the Privacy Officer within 24 hours by emailing privacy@northsidefamilyclinic.example. The sender must not try to resolve the problem alone.

## P01-1.5 Text messaging
Staff must not send patient information by text message from personal phones. Only the clinic-approved secure messaging application may be used for patient-related messages.
