# P03: Access Control and Authentication Policy
Northside Family Clinic (fictional organization; synthetic policy written for a course project, not legal advice)

## P03-3.1 Unique user accounts
Every user must have a unique user ID. Shared accounts and shared passwords are prohibited on all clinic systems.

## P03-3.2 Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases are encouraged. Passwords are not changed on a fixed schedule, but a password must be changed immediately if compromise is suspected. Passwords must never be written down or shared.

## P03-3.3 Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for clinic email, the electronic health record (EHR) system, the VPN and any other remote access to clinic systems.

## P03-3.4 Role-based access and least privilege
Access to systems and records is granted according to job role and is limited to what the person needs to do their job. For example, billing staff may view billing information but may not open clinical notes.

## P03-3.5 Access reviews and termination
Managers review the access of their team members every quarter. When an employee's employment ends, IT must remove all of that person's system access within 4 hours of the termination.

## P03-3.6 Session timeouts
Workstations lock automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity. The EHR system logs users out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
