# P04: Remote Work and Mobile Device Policy
Northside Family Clinic (fictional organization; synthetic policy written for a course project, not legal advice)

## P04-4.1 Approved devices
Only clinic-managed laptops may be used to access the EHR system from outside the clinic. Personal devices must not store ePHI. A personal device may reach clinic applications only through the clinic's virtual desktop, and downloading files to the personal device is not allowed.

## P04-4.2 VPN
The clinic VPN must be active whenever a clinic-managed device is used on any network outside the clinic.

## P04-4.3 Public Wi-Fi
Staff must not use public Wi-Fi networks, such as in cafes or airports, to reach clinic systems unless the VPN is connected first.

## P04-4.4 Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen laptop, phone or storage device that holds or can reach clinic data must be reported to the IT Help Desk within 1 hour of discovery. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device.

## P04-4.5 Printing at home
Printing patient information at home is prohibited.

## P04-4.6 Encryption
Full-disk encryption is required on every laptop and mobile phone that holds or can access clinic data.
