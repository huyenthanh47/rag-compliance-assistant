# P06: Patient Access to Health Records Policy
Northside Family Clinic (fictional organization; synthetic policy written for a course project, not legal advice)

## P06-6.1 Right of access
Patients have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of their health records. A request may be made in writing or through the patient portal.

## P06-6.2 Response time
The clinic must respond to a records request within 30 calendar days of receiving it. The clinic may take one extension of up to 30 additional days if it gives the patient written notice of the reason for the delay within the first 30 days.

## P06-6.3 Identity verification
Before releasing records, staff must verify the requester's identity with a government-issued photo ID or by confirming a patient portal login.

## P06-6.4 Fees
The clinic may charge only a reasonable, cost-based fee for copying. There is no fee for viewing records in the patient portal.

## P06-6.5 Release to another person
Records may be released to a third party, such as a family member or attorney, only with a signed authorization on Form NFC-12.

## P06-6.6 Denial of access
Access may be denied only by a licensed clinician and only when the clinician determines that access is likely to cause substantial harm. The patient must be told in writing and may request a review of the decision.
