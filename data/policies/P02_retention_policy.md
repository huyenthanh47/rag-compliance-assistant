# P02: Data Retention and Disposal Policy
Northside Family Clinic (fictional organization; synthetic policy written for a course project, not legal advice)

## P02-2.1 Patient medical records
Medical records of adult patients are retained for 10 years after the date of the patient's last service. Records of minors are retained until the patient reaches age 21, or for 10 years after the last service, whichever period is longer.

## P02-2.2 Compliance documentation
Compliance documents, including policies, training records, risk assessments and incident reports, are retained for 6 years from the date they were created or last in effect, whichever is later.

## P02-2.3 Audit and access logs
System audit logs and access logs are kept readily available for 12 months and then moved to archive storage. Archived logs are retained so that the total retention period is 6 years.

## P02-2.4 Disposal
Paper records must be destroyed with a cross-cut shredder or placed in a locked shred bin. Electronic media must be wiped following NIST SP 800-88 guidelines or physically destroyed. IT records every disposal in the disposal log and keeps the destruction certificate.

## P02-2.5 Legal hold
When the Compliance Officer issues a legal hold, no record covered by the hold may be destroyed, regardless of the retention periods above, until the Compliance Officer lifts the hold in writing.
